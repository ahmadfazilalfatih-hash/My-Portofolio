# Design System: Clean Ice-Blue Editorial Bento Slide-Deck

## Brand & Visual Identity
- **Owner**: Ahmad Fazil Al Fatih
- **Concept**: Clean Ice-Blue & Royal Navy Editorial Slide-Deck Bento Grid
- **Mood**: Crisp, architectural, airy, academic, tech-forward yet artistic.

---

## 1. Color Palette & Tokens

### Primary & Neutral Tokens
- `--bg-outer`: `#5C83B8` (Soft slate-cerulean studio backdrop with diagonal window sunlight gradient)
- `--slide-bg-start`: `#F7FAFF`
- `--slide-bg-mid`: `#DFECFD`
- `--slide-bg-end`: `#C4DCFA`
- `--slide-bg`: `linear-gradient(135deg, #F7FAFF 0%, #DFECFD 55%, #C4DCFA 100%)`
- `--navy-primary`: `#092147` (Deep midnight navy for primary display headings, text, and crisp outlines)
- `--royal-blue`: `#1D63C6` (Vibrant royal blue for accents, corner waves, timeline nodes, and highlights)
- `--sky-wave`: `#8EB8F0` (Translucent soft periwinkle-blue layered wave contours)
- `--tile-blue`: `#CFE2FC` (Soft pastel ice-blue inner rounded tiles for services and tags)
- `--ink`: `#092147` (Main copy in high emphasis)
- `--ink-soft`: `#334E75` (Secondary body text and subtitles)
- `--white`: `#FFFFFF`

### Accent & Skill Colors
- Python & Logic: `#102A43` / `#FBBF24`
- Inkscape Vector: `#0F2922` / `#34D399`
- Canva Design: `linear-gradient(135deg, #00C4CC, #7D2AE8)`
- CapCut Video: `#111827` / `#FFFFFF`
- Arabic Editorial: `#1D4ED8` / `#DBEAFE`

---

## 2. Typography Hierarchy

### Font Families
- **Display & Headings**: `'Poppins', sans-serif` (Weights: 700, 800, 900)
- **Body & Secondary UI**: `'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif` (Weights: 500, 600, 700)
- **Signature Brush Calligraphy**: `'Alex Brush', cursive`
- **Handwritten Notes**: `'Caveat', cursive` (Weight: 600)

### Type Scale
- **Hero Display Title**: `clamp(64px, 11.5vw, 152px)` | Poppins 900 | Letter spacing: `-0.05em` | Line-height: `0.95`
- **Hero Overlaid Signature**: `clamp(42px, 6.2vw, 82px)` | Alex Brush | Color: `#FFFFFF` | Text shadow: `0 4px 18px rgba(9, 33, 71, 0.35)`
- **Section Heading (Bento Card)**: `28px` | Poppins 800 | Color: `#092147`
- **Sub-headings / Card Titles**: `14px - 16px` | Plus Jakarta Sans 700 / 800 | `#092147`
- **Body Text**: `13px - 14px` | Plus Jakarta Sans 500 | `#1E3A63` | Line-height: `1.65`
- **Captions & Metadata**: `11px - 12px` | Plus Jakarta Sans 600 | `#334E75`
- **Handwritten Accent Note**: `22px` | Caveat 600 / Alex Brush | Color: `#092147` / `#1D63C6`

## 4. Signature Graphic Motifs & Component Patterns
1. **Layered Corner Waves**: Royal blue (`#1D63C6`) and soft periwinkle (`#8EB8F0`) organic vectors nestled into card corners with thin white stroke accents.
2. **Hand-Drawn Doodles**:
   - 4-point sparkle star (`✦`)
   - Double-heart outline (`♡`)
   - Three-droplet splash (`\ | /`)
   - Curved underline brush stroke
3. **Circular Social / Contact Badges**: Solid `#092147` circles (`26px` x `26px`) with white centered icons.
4. **Interactive Bento Components**:
   - Arched photo preview with hover upload trigger.
   - 3-column square project showcases with click-to-modal behavior.
   - Filterable vertical timeline for experience, education, and achievements.
   - 4-column pastel blue service blocks.
